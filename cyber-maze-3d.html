How To Play

  1. Choose a Grid Size with the slider (7×7 easy → 61×61 extreme).
  2. Pick a mode:
    • Maze Solver — WASD/Arrows to navigate top-down. Reach the top-right exit.
    • Maze Runner — First-person FPS. WASD + mouse to escape. Click to lock cursor.
  3. Ghost AI chases you using real-time A* / BFS / Greedy pathfinding (switch in the AI Brain Deck).
  4. Abilities:
    • SPACE — EMP Blast: stuns the Ghost for 3.5s
    • E — Spirit Decoy: lures AI to the exit corner
  5. Collect glowing Data Cores scattered through the maze for bonus score.
  6. Press TAB to open the AI Brain Deck — live telemetry & algorithm switcher.

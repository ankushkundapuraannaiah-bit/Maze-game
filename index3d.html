How To Play

  1. Choose a Difficulty or Grid Size with the slider (7×7 easy → 61×61 extreme).
  2. Pick a mode:
    • Chase Cam — Cinematic third-person follow. WASD/Arrows to navigate.
    • First Person — Immersive 3D runner. WASD + mouse to look around and sprint. Click canvas to lock cursor.
  3. Nemesis AI pursues you using real-time A* / BFS / Greedy pathfinding (toggle in the AI Brain Deck).
  4. Tactical Abilities:
    • SPACE — EMP Blast: 8-tile radius shockwave that paralyzes the Nemesis AI for up to 4.5s.
    • E — Hologram Decoy: deploys a decoy that diverts the AI's pathfinding target.
  5. Collect glowing Data Cores scattered through the maze for score multipliers.
  6. Press TAB to open the AI Brain Deck — live telemetry & algorithm switcher.
